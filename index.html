DialogueDirector — Listening Test

We're testing how robust our dialogue generation is to noisy speaker references. Each question has two reference recordings of the speakers (one or both may contain background noise — hiss, hum, reverb, etc.) followed by two generated dialogues, (a) and (b).

Your job is to identify which generation sounds closer to the speakers’ clean voice — without the background noise — and which one has copied the noise from the reference (which is what we’re trying to avoid). Pick the cleaner, more faithful sample. If they sound about equally clean (or equally noisy), choose “About the same”.

Please use headphones in a quiet environment. Each session shows a small random subset of the full evaluation set, and the order of (a) and (b) is randomized per session.
